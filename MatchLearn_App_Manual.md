# MatchLearn App User Manual

**App version:** 1.0.0  
**Audience:** MatchLearn students  
**Platform:** Android and iOS

MatchLearn helps students find study buddies and groups, communicate, and plan study sessions. This guide explains the app’s main features and how to use them.

## 1. Getting started

### Sign in or create an account

1. Open MatchLearn and tap **Sign in with Google**. Use the verified SSCT or SNSU Google account linked to your MatchLearn account.
2. To create a new account, tap **Create account**, enter your full name, tap **Next**, then continue with Google using a verified `@ssct.edu.ph` or `@snsu.edu.ph` email address.
3. Your confirmed name is used for a new MatchLearn profile. Existing account names are kept as saved.
4. Complete your study profile so MatchLearn can provide relevant matches.

Google is used for both sign-in and account creation; MatchLearn does not require a password.

## 2. Complete your profile

New or incomplete profiles are guided through a three-step setup: personal information, academic information, and study match preferences. Save each step to continue. Required information is highlighted in red; while setup is incomplete, leaving the profile for another app section prompts you to return and finish. Required matching information includes a department, program, year level, preferred study time, study mode, study style, at least one available day, at least one strength, and at least one weakness. Courses are optional, but the course field stays highlighted until at least one is added. Follow the displayed `COURSE CODE - Course Name` format (for example, `IT 213 - Database Management Systems`). Personal information requires a full name. Phone number, address, gender, marital status, bio, and student ID are optional.

After setup is complete, you can edit your profile at any time from the **Profile** tab.

The profile has three sections:

- **Profile:** Add or update your name, photo, bio, phone number, address, and other personal details. Phone number and address have visibility controls. Keep their visibility private unless you want other students to see them.
- **Academic:** Select your department, program, and year level.
- **Study Match:** Add your courses, availability, preferred time and study mode, learning style, academic strengths, and areas where you would like help.

Tap **Save Profile Changes** to save. A more complete and accurate study profile helps MatchLearn identify compatible students. If you try to leave with unsaved changes, review the confirmation before discarding them.

## 3. Navigate the app

Use the bottom navigation bar to open the main areas:

| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Dashboard, recommended matches, and shortcuts to sessions, reviews, and notifications. |
| **Discover** | Browse recommended study buddies or groups. |
| **Study Sessions** | Create, review, and manage study-session invitations and your schedule. |
| **Messages** | Open direct and group conversations. |
| **Buddies** | Manage accepted buddies, study groups, and incoming or sent requests. |
| **Profile** | Edit your personal, academic, and study-match information and account settings. |

Badges on tabs indicate new matches, pending session invitations, unread messages, or incoming buddy requests. A badge may clear when you open the corresponding area.

When a student has ratings, their avatar in Discover, Buddies, study sessions, messages, and reviews shows a compact badge with their average score and number of ratings. Your own photos in **Profile** are not badged.

## 4. Find study buddies and groups

1. Open **Discover**.
2. Select **Study buddies** or **Study groups**.
3. Use the search box to find people by name, course, or skills, or groups by name, course, or schedule.
4. Pull down to refresh recommendations if needed.

### View a study-buddy match

Tap a person to view their available profile information, compatibility score, match reasons, and ratings.

- Tap **Request to be study buddies** to send a connection request.
- If the other person sent you a request, accept it from the match screen or from **Buddies → Requests**.
- After a connection is accepted, you can open a direct conversation with **Message study buddy**.

The compatibility score is based on study-profile information. Treat it as a recommendation, not a guarantee that a study partnership will be a good fit.

### Request to join a group

1. In **Discover**, select **Study groups**.
2. Open a group to review its information.
3. Send a join request.
4. Wait for a group administrator to approve it. You can review requests and their status in **Buddies → Requests**.

## 5. Manage buddies and groups

Open **Buddies** to switch among the available buddy, group, and request views. Use the search box to locate people, groups, or requests.

### Buddy requests

- In **Requests**, tap **Accept** to connect or **Decline** to reject an incoming request.
- Requests you sent remain marked as pending until the recipient responds.
- Open an accepted buddy to view their profile, message them, review them, or manage the connection.

### Study groups

- Tap **Create a study group** to set a group name and course, choose an icon and color, and select study preferences. A schedule can be added where available.
- As a group administrator, review incoming join requests in **Buddies → Requests**, then accept or decline them.
- Open a group to view its details and conversation. Group administrators can manage group details. Members can leave a group; if an administrator leaves a group with other members, the app asks them to choose a replacement administrator.

Use the report and safety options on a buddy’s detail page if you need to report a concern.

## 6. Plan study sessions

1. Open **Study Sessions** and tap **Create session**.
2. Choose a paired study buddy or a group you have joined.
3. Enter a topic, start and end date/time, and location.
4. Tap **Send invitation**. The other participant or group members may need to respond before the session is confirmed.

Use the **All**, **On going**, **Pending**, **Completed**, and **Cancelled** filters to find sessions. Switch between list and calendar views. The calendar displays confirmed sessions; select a date to see its agenda.

Session date colors show timing or completion:

| Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Blue | At least 14 days away |
| Green | 8–13 days away |
| Yellow | 4–7 days away |
| Orange | 1–3 days away |
| Red | Today |
| Purple | Past |
| Gray | Completed |

The color is a quick visual guide; check the session’s status and date details before taking action. Available actions depend on your role and the session status. The creator can edit or delete a session; participants can respond to invitations and manage their own session list.

## 7. Send messages

1. Open **Messages**.
2. Search for a conversation or tap a buddy or group chat.
3. Type your message in the box at the bottom and tap **Send**.

Messages support text and clickable web links. The current app does not provide document, image, or other file attachments in chat. Only open links you trust.

## 8. Notifications and reviews

### Notifications

Open **Notifications** from the Home screen to review activity such as buddy requests, group requests, and study-session updates. Tap an unread item to mark it as read, or choose **Mark all as read**. Pull down to refresh.

### Reviews

Open **Reviews** from the Home screen or a buddy’s details to see ratings and feedback. You can rate a study buddy after completing at least one study session together. Select the buddy, choose an overall star rating, optionally add a comment, and submit. You can update your rating later.

## 9. Account and privacy

Open **Profile** and then **Settings** to access account and app controls. Use Google sign-in to access your account.

### Privacy and personal information

Use the visibility options in the personal profile to decide whether your phone number and address are shown to other students. Avoid placing sensitive information in your bio or messages. For formal privacy questions or data requests, contact the app administrator or your institution.

### Sign out or delete your account

- Choose **Sign Out** in Profile to end your current session. You will need to sign in again to use the app.
- Choose **Delete account** only if you intend to permanently remove your account. Confirm the deletion prompt carefully. If you are unsure about account or data deletion, contact the app administrator first.

## 10. Troubleshooting and safe use

- **Google sign-in is unavailable:** Confirm you are using the Android or iOS app and that Google sign-in is configured for the installed build. Contact the app administrator if the configuration message remains.
- **Your profile has no matches:** Add your program, year level, courses, study preferences, strengths or learning needs, and availability. Then refresh **Discover**.
- **A join or buddy request is still pending:** The recipient or group administrator must respond. Check **Buddies → Requests**.
- **A screen fails to load:** Check your internet connection and try refreshing or using **Try again**. If the problem continues, contact the app administrator.
- **Protect your privacy:** Share only information you are comfortable showing to other students. Keep contact details private unless you intentionally choose to share them.
- **Plan meetings responsibly:** Confirm a session’s time and location with the participants. Follow your institution’s guidance for meeting people and reporting safety concerns.

MatchLearn supports student discovery and coordination. It does not replace your institution’s official communication, learning, or emergency-support channels.
